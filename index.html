About FLOWRA

Founded in 2026, FLOWRA is built around a simple idea: banking should be easy, flexible, secure, and designed to help people move forward. We provide financial solutions that support everyday spending, responsible borrowing, and long-term financial growth.

OUR MISSION

Empowerment with Confidence

To provide simple, secure, and flexible financial solutions that empower individuals to manage their money with confidence. Through convenient banking services and card options designed for different needs, FLOWRA aims to help customers make smarter financial decisions and achieve their goals.

OUR VISION

A Trusted Community Bank

To become a trusted and innovative community bank where everyone has access to financial solutions that fit their lifestyle, helping them spend wisely, borrow responsibly, and grow confidently toward a better future.

Support & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Flowra Bank?

Flowra is an independent, member-owned mutual financial and time-exchange network combining Peso savings with a reciprocal skill-sharing ledger.

Where is the FLOWRA Main Office located?

Our main operations and customer desk are located at Salvacion, Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya.

What are the costs for physical FLOWRA cards?

Card issuance fees are: ₱250 for Debit, ₱300 for Platinum Credit, and ₱400 for Executive Charge Credit. All card fees must be settled via Online Payment only.

Can I pick up my card at the main office?

Yes. Upon ordering your card online, you can select in-person pick-up at the FLOWRA Main Office, Salvacion, Bayombong, or request door-to-door delivery.
